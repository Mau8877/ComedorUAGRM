Here is a short explanation of what this site stores in your browser and how you can delete it.

## 1. What a cookie is

A cookie is a small file a site stores in your browser to remember things between visits. There is also local storage (`localStorage`), which does a similar job.

## 2. What this site uses

This site **does not use analytics or advertising cookies**. It only uses essential local storage to remember two of your preferences:

- **Theme**: whether you prefer light or dark mode.
- **Language**: the language of the page you visited, which comes from the address (`/es/` or `/en/`).

This data stays on your device and is not sent to any server. Because it is essential for the site to work the way you ask, it does not need a consent banner.

## 3. How to delete it

You can remove the site's storage from your browser settings (privacy section, "site data"). The next time you visit, the site goes back to the theme your system indicates.

## 4. Future changes

If we ever add analytics or advertising, we will update this policy and show a banner asking for your consent before turning them on.
