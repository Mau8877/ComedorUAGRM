This policy explains what personal data Nexware handles when you use this site, what we use it for, and what you can do with it.

## 1. What data we collect

We only collect what you type in the contact form: your **name**, your **email address** and your **message**. In this demo version the form does not send the data to any server.

## 2. What we use it for

We use that data only to answer your inquiry and, if you ask, to prepare a quote. We do not use it for advertising and we do not sell it.

## 3. Basis and retention

We handle your data because you give it to us voluntarily so we can reply. We keep it for as long as the conversation lasts and a reasonable time afterwards to handle follow-up questions; then it is deleted.

## 4. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data at any time by writing to [ceo.desarrollo@nexware.example](mailto:ceo.desarrollo@nexware.example). We will reply within a reasonable time.

## 5. Who we share it with

We do not share your data with third parties, except with the hosting provider if the site is deployed on a service such as AWS, which processes it only to provide the hosting service.

## 6. Security

We apply reasonable measures to protect information: encrypted connections (HTTPS), restricted access and periodic review. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute protection.

## 7. Minors

The site is not aimed at minors and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you think a minor wrote to us, let us know and we will delete it.

## 8. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Updated on" date shows the latest revision.

## 9. Contact

For any privacy question: [ceo.desarrollo@nexware.example](mailto:ceo.desarrollo@nexware.example).
